Marble Drift is a tilt-maze game for Android. Guide a marble through mazes by tilting your phone, beat the clock and collect stars. Built for Solana phones.

Guest mode works offlineNo adsNo trackingPay with SOL, SKR or USDC

🎮 Two ways to play

Guest: a short offline world, no wallet and no internet. Full game: connect a Solana wallet for all worlds, saved on a server so you never lose progress.

📅 Daily Maze

A new maze every day, the same for every player. Keep your streak going and earn special marbles.

🔮 Marbles

Collect marbles from streak gifts, or buy more in the shop.

🔐 Your wallet stays yours

The game never sees your private keys. Every payment is approved in your own wallet app.

Payments

Shop items (a heart refill, streak freezes and marbles) are paid in SOL, SKR or USDC on the Solana blockchain. The price is set in US dollars by the game's server, converted at the current rate, and the item is delivered only after the server has checked your payment on the blockchain. See the terms for details.

Privacy

Guest mode keeps everything on your phone. The full game stores your progress under your wallet address. Read the full privacy policy.
